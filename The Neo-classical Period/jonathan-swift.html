2. Jonathan Swift

Jonathan Swift (1667–1745) was an Irish satirist, essayist, and poet, best known for his works "Gulliver's Travels" and "A Modest Proposal".

A Voyage to Lilliput

a voyage to the land of the Houyhnhnms, a race of intelligent horses, and the Yahoos, a degenerate human-like species. The novel is a satirical critique of human nature and society.

A Voyage to Brobdingnag

a voyage to the land of the Brobdingnags, a race of giant humans. The novel is a satirical critique of human nature and society.

A Voyage to Laputa, Balnibarbi, Luggnagg, Glubbdubdrib and Japan

a voyage to laputa, giant flying island, and other lands. The novel is a satirical critique of human nature and society.
