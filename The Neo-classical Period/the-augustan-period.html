The Augustan Period (1700–1745)

Notable events of this period:

  • Actually there was no king in England called Augustan.
  • Also called Age of Pope according to the famous poet Alexander Pope.
  • In 1707, Scotland and England unite to form Great Britain.
Important literary figures of the Augustan period:

  • 1. Alexander Pope

  • 2. Jonathan Swift

  • 3. Daniel Defoe

  • 4. Samuel Richardson

  • 5. Joseph Addison and Richard Steele
