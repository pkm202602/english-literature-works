The Neo-Classical Period (1660–1785/1798)

There are three main sub-periods in the Neo-Classical era:

  • The Restoration Period (1660–1700)

  • The Augustan Period (1700–1750)

  • The Age of Sensibility (1750–1798)

This age also called 17th & 18th centuries in English literary history.
