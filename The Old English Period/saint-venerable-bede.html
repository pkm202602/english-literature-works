3. Saint Venerable Bede

  • Saint Venerable Bede was given the title of 'Doctor of the Church' and 'Father of Learning'.
  • He is called the 'first historian in the English language'."(Note: The first historical book in Bengali literature is 'Banga Bhasa o Sahitya' by Dinesh Chandra Sen.).
  • .
  • He wrote in Latin, which was the scholarly language of the Anglo-Saxon era.
