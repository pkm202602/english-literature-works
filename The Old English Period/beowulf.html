5. Poet of Beowulf

First Monument in English Literature:Beowulf

  • This epic was written by an unknown Anglo-Saxon poet. Scholars recognize him as the author of Beowulf. This work is the earliest sign of English literature, also known as the 'First Monument in English Literature' [like Charyapada in Bengali].
  • It is also called The Earliest Epic in England. It was probably written in 650. (For example: The first and most successful epic in Bengali literature is Michael Madhusudan's Meghnad Badh-1861) .
  • Although set in Scandinavia, the epic poem is recognized as England's national epic poem. It is considered the first important example in the traditional history of English literature.
  • This Heroic Epic, written in Old English for entertainment, had 3182 lines. The hero of the epic is named Beowulf, who first (in his youth) saved England from the demons of Patalpuri. Later (in his later years) he himself died while saving it from dragons. The original pages of this book are preserved in the British Museum in London.
  • Summary of Beowulf:
  • The main character of the epic, Beowulf, is a warrior of the Getae people. He comes to help the Danish king Hrothgar. Hrothgar's great palace, Heort, was being attacked by a giant named Grendel. Beowulf kills Grendel with his bare hands. Then, with the help of a sword found in the giant's lair, he kills Grendel's mother. In his later life, Beowulf becomes king of the Getae. A dragon begins to invade his kingdom. The dragon's treasure, which was kept in a tomb, was stolen. With the help of his servants, Beowulf attacks the dragon. But they are unsuccessful. Beowulf follows the dragon to its home in Arnanis. No one wants to go with him. Only his young Swedish relative Wiglaf goes with him. Finally, Beowulf is able to kill the dragon. But he is seriously injured in the battle and dies. He was buried in a tomb by the sea. .
  • Apart from Beowulf, there are some important poems called The Wanderer, The Seafarer, The Husband's Message, The Wife's Lament, Traveler, etc. No specific author is known for these.
