4. King Alfred the Great

  • King Alfred the Great was given the title of 'The Law Governing'.
  • He was the King of England from 871 to 899"( .).
  • He compiled the Anglo Saxon Chronicle.( which means the first prose work was compiled in this era.)
  • It is First monument in English prose.(The first prose of Bengali literature is 'Crepar Xaxtrer Orth, Bhed', which was compiled by the Portuguese priest Manuel da Assumpção.)
  • For this reason, he is also called the Father of English Prose (just as Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar is the Father of Bengali Prose).
  • King Alfred the Great spread educational institutions in this period.

Keep in mind: In 450 AD, the fierce Saxons of Germany not only conquered England but also imposed a sort of ban on practicing the English language. They enslaved the poor English people and banished the nobles from the country. As a result, no literature was written in pure English during this era.
